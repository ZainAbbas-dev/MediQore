## Trying out MediQore, a mobile app for Lady Health Workers

You are invited to try out a mobile app and tell us what you think of it. Please read this sheet, or ask us to read it to you, before you decide. You may ask any question at any time.

**Who is doing this?** Muhammad Zain Abbas and Zain Ali, final-year BS Computer Science students at COMSATS University Islamabad. Their supervisor is Ma'am Sajida Kalsoom. The university's Institutional Ethical Committee approved this activity on [date], reference [number].

**What is the app?** MediQore is a student project. It is an Urdu mobile app for recording the work of Lady Health Workers: registering pregnant women, home visits, emergency alerts, and child health and polio work. It works without internet. It can also suggest a risk level for a pregnant woman, but this is only a help for the health worker. It is not a medical diagnosis.

**Why are you invited?** You work as a Lady Health Worker or a supervisor of Lady Health Workers. Your opinion tells us whether the app is easy to read, hear and use.

**What will happen if you take part?**

1. We will ask a few general questions: your age group, years of service, and how often you use a smartphone. We will not write your name on these answers.
2. You will do some short tasks on our test phone, for example registering a woman and recording a visit. **All the patients in the app are made up.** You will not enter or see any real patient's information.
3. You will answer a short questionnaire about the app and tell us what you liked and what was difficult.

It takes about 45–60 minutes in total. [A female team member will be present.] [We will not make any audio or video recording.]

**Is it compulsory?** No. Taking part is your free choice.

- Saying no will not affect your job in any way, and we will not tell your supervisor or the health department who took part.
- You can stop at any time without giving a reason.
- You can ask us to remove your answers until [date].

**Are there any risks?** The main cost is your time. Some tasks may feel difficult. That shows us where the app needs improving; we are testing the app, not you.

**What happens to the information?**

- Your answers are kept under a code number, not your name.
- Your signed consent form is kept separately in a locked place, and only the two students and their supervisor can see the information.
- The results appear in the students' final-year report only as totals and anonymous comments, never with names.
- The information is destroyed after [period].

**Will you benefit?** There is no direct benefit or payment [Decide: refreshments / travel costs will be provided]. Your views help make a tool that fits the work of Lady Health Workers.

**Questions or concerns?** Contact [supervisor's name, official email and phone]. If you have a concern about how this activity is run, you can contact the COMSATS University Islamabad Institutional Ethical Committee at [committee contact].

Please keep this sheet.
